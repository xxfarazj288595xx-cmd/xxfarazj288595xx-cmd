## Selected projects

One feature I built is a **vehicle jump starter finder**. Shoppers select their car, and the tool explains why each product is recommended or ruled out. [Visit RAYABUY](https://rayabuy.com/)

### Quadrotor · Inertia Tensor and Rod-Tip Kinematics

I modeled a quadrotor and an attached rod as a rigid body. I calculated the inertia tensor as a function of the rod’s mounting angle and expressed it about both the attachment point and the combined center of mass. The off-diagonal term from the tilted rod makes the coupling between the body axes explicit.

**Methods** · Component mass properties, coordinate transformations, parallel-axis theorem, and rigid-body kinematics.

### Silicon PN Junction · Depletion-Region Model

I used Python to visualize charge density, electric field, and electrostatic potential across abrupt silicon PN junctions. I compared symmetric doping with p⁺–n and p–n⁺ cases to show how the depletion region extends farther into the more lightly doped side.

**Tools** · Python, NumPy, and Matplotlib.

### Diode Circuits · OrCAD / PSpice

I simulated diode I–V characteristics, half-wave and full-wave bridge rectifiers, and Zener circuits. I compared the simulations with hand calculations and documented which values came directly from the PSpice cursors.

**Tools** · OrCAD Capture and PSpice A/D.
